## Brand & Style

This design system powers a high-octane, competitive digital art arena. It marries the sleek discipline of contemporary creative software with the charged tension of live creative battle. The emotional target is electric focus: giving artists a studio-grade workspace while charging audiences with tournament-level stakes and adrenaline.

The aesthetic leans into a refined **Dark Modern Tech / Glassmorphic** fusion. It relies on a backdrop of deep slate charcoal, architectural surface layering, and surgical strokes of energetic neon orange to convey heat, live states, and critical actions. Transparent frosted planes offer depth without distracting from high-fidelity digital artwork, while crisp geometric borders ensure every asset, canvas, and submission feels framed and museum-grade.

## Colors

The palette establishes an obsidian stage engineered to showcase dynamic, multi-spectral user artwork with absolute fidelity.

- **Primary (`#FF6B00` / `#F97316`):** Kinetic Orange. Reserved for competition states, live voting actions, primary CTAs, active timers, and podium rankings. Tertiary orange acts as a hover/active glow and subtle gradient terminus.
- **Secondary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for headline typography, active icons, and high-priority indicators.
- **Surface Architecture:**
  - Base Canvas / Scrim: `#1F1F1F` to `#242424` for overall page body depth.
  - Background Floor: `#353535` for primary app framing and structural panes.
  - Surface Containers: `#3F3F3F` for floating toolbars, modals, and panel shelves.
  - Card Surfaces: `#2C2C2C` to preserve contrast behind artwork thumbnails.
- **Borders & Rims:** `#4B5563` and `#525252` applied at low opacities (`0.4` to `0.8`) to define crisp structural boundaries against dark backdrops.
- **Muted Text & Metadata:** `#D1D5DB` (Body Secondary) and `#9CA3AF` (Captions/Meta).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes three specialized type families to reflect competition rigor, digital precision, and content legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a technological, experimental edge. Tightly tracked letter spacing lends dynamic urgency to competition themes, bracket match-ups, and artist titles.
- **Body & Editorial (`Work Sans`):** Clean, neutral, and readable. It yields effortless scanning across artist bios, competition rules, and multi-threaded community critiques.
- **Labels, Metrics & Badges (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced precision chosen specifically for real-time countdown clocks, poly-counts, layer metrics, vote tallies, and arena ranks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column fluid grid system, adapting down to 8 columns on tablet devices (768px - 1024px) and 4 columns on mobile viewports (<768px). 

Layout rules prioritize visual breathing room around art assets:
- **Canvas Showcase Modules:** Expand to full bleed or lock into 16:9 and 1:1 viewports with inner component margins preserved at `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- **Battle Arena / Split Canvas:** 50/50 dual-column splits on desktop collapse into stacked vertical cards with an anchored middle-pinned match controller on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Dense utility toolbars and inspector palettes employ tight spacing tokens (`space-xs`, `space-sm`), while public gallery walls and match lobbies scale up to `space-lg` and `space-xl` to prevent sensory clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through a hybrid approach combining tonal depth layering, frosted glass overlays, and luminous orange ambient backlights:

- **Level 0 (Stage/Base):** Flat slate `#1F1F1F` to `#353535`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Raised `#2C2C2C` with a 1px perimeter stroke using `rgba(82, 82, 82, 0.6)`. Shadows are suppressed in favor of clean edge contrast.
- **Level 2 (Floating Floating Palettes, Glass Headers, Modals):** Background tint `#3F3F3F` at 80% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner 1px top highlight using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Spotlight & Active Match States):** Radiant ambient glow. Elements feature an outer colored drop shadow: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The geometric structure relies on balanced `0.5rem` (8px) baseline curves for containers, creating an intentional contrast between soft, ergonomic framing and hard-angled digital precision.

- **Cards & Modal Containers:** Follow the standard `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) for an architectural shell.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Standard `0.5rem` (8px) curvature for a tactile, modern software feel.
- **Status Tags, Badges & Counter Pills:** Zero radius (`0px`) or micro radius (`2px`) to deliberately create sharp, competition-grade markers that distinguish system meta-data from user interface buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#FF6B00`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `JetBrains Mono` or `Space Grotesk` medium. Crisp 1px outline of `#F97316` on hover with a `0 0 16px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#3F3F3F` surface hover, framed by a 1px `#4B5563` border. Text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Destructive / Danger:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, 1px border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`, text `#EF4444`.

### Cards & Submissions
- Background `#2C2C2C` with a 1px border of `#4B5563` (50% alpha). Rounded corners at `1rem`. 
- Artworks fill the top container cleanly. An integrated frosted glass gradient overlay sits on hover, revealing voting triggers, artist metadata, and duel statistics.

### Badges & Match Tags
- Engineered as sharp, high-contrast rectangles (`rounded: 0px`).
- Monospaced typography (`label-sm`).
- Live Battle: Background `#FF6B00`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Match Status: Background `rgba(75, 85, 99, 0.3)`, border `1px solid #525252`, text `#D1D5DB`.

### Form Inputs & Sliders
- Input fields use `#262626` background, inset 1px border `#4B5563`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#9CA3AF`. Focused state switches border to `#FF6B00` with an amber focus ring.
- Range and brush sliders feature a solid `#FF6B00` accent fill track over an unselected `#3F3F3F` track.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox base with subtle 2px rounding; radio is fully circular. Surface `#262626`, border `#525252`.
- Checked state transitions immediately to `#FF6B00` with crisp white iconography.

### Specialized Components: Duel / Bracket Match Bar
- Pinned header showing live artist avatars against opposing sides.
- Central countdown timer with `JetBrains Mono` and dual percentage vote meters that dynamically push orange versus neutral-white gradients across the top axis.
